## Cross to reLATTE

reLATTE checks out a **pinned source commit** of the GHoT proposer from its own runner, invokes it with explicit synthetic priorities, validates the manifest, and includes the offer's digest in an admitted Minecraft runtime instance. A single operator action injects the selected fault; a server and fresh independent protocol observer must agree on the trace.

**Crucial new rule**: the two worlds need **not produce identical causal signatures**. They need to agree with their respective externally supplied, bounded *expectations*. Their differences are a result to preserve, not a disagreement to erase.

Each candidate still arrives in GHoT's sovereign inbox as `HOLD`, under existing reLATTE cryptographic checks. External ephemeral signers are *not* organizational roots of trust.
